How to choose

Airflow
Typical models are around 1.3–1.5 m³/min, high-airflow models around 2 m³/min. The higher the airflow, the shorter the drying time and the less time your hair is exposed to heat.
Temperature switching
Air that is too hot strains hair and scalp. A low-temperature mode or hot/cool switching lets you adapt for finishing and for summer.
Weight and shape
You use it every day, so its weight and balance in the hand decide how happy you are with it. If you put it away, a foldable model.
Ion-type features
Features such as nanoe are each maker’s own technology, and how much difference people notice varies. Compare airflow and temperature control first, and treat extra features as a matter of preference.
Power
Most are around 1200 W, and using one together with other appliances can trip the breaker.

Watch out for

  • Dust building up on the air intake reduces airflow and can cause overheating. Clean it regularly.
  • Wrapping the cord around the body for storage can break the wires inside.
  • Using it outside Japan: Made for Japan’s 100 V mains, and the manufacturer supports use in Japan only. 110–120 V is above its rating and can cause overheating or failure; never use it on 220–240 V.

How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n) … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50

So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.

Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
