As a guide, 3 go for one person, 5.5 go for 2–4 people and 1 shō (10 go) for 5 or more. For the lowest price, a microcomputer model; for better-cooked rice, IH; for a chewier, stickier texture, pressure IH. At the top end, the differences are the inner pot’s material and heating power.

## How to choose (as of 2026-10)

- **Capacity**: If you usually cook about half the maximum capacity, a size smaller cooks more evenly.
- **Heating method**: Microcomputer models heat with an element at the bottom and are cheap. IH heats the whole pot and is more powerful. Pressure IH cooks at a higher temperature under pressure, which tends to give chewier rice.
- **Inner pot**: Thick pots and materials such as clay, iron or copper change how well heat is held. Most of the price difference lies here.
- **Cleaning**: The fewer parts you have to wash every time (inner lid, steam vent), the easier it is to keep up.
- **Keep warm**: Keeping rice warm for a long time tends to yellow and dry it. If you freeze your rice, prioritise how it cooks over how it keeps warm.

### Watch out for

- A damaged inner-pot coating costs money to replace. Check the price of a replacement inner pot too.
- **Using it outside Japan**: Made for Japan’s 100 V mains, and the manufacturer supports use in Japan only; never use it on 220–240 V. Japanese makers also sell separate models made for overseas voltages.

## How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

- Formula: `score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n)  … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50`
- Why: So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.
- Price check: Prices are recorded daily and compared only with days on the same shipping terms. Under 7 days observed: insufficient_data. At the lowest observed price and at least 3% under the median: lowest_observed. At or under 90% of the median: below_usual. At or over 110%: above_usual. Otherwise usual. The window is up to 90 days and reported as window_days.
- Note: Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
