How to choose

Capacity (mAh)
Conversion losses mean only about 60–70% of the rated capacity reaches the phone. 10,000 mAh charges a typical smartphone about 1.5–2 times.
Output (W)
Fast-charging an iPhone needs USB PD 20 W or more. A laptop needs close to the wattage of its own adapter (45–65 W or more).
Weight
About 180–250 g for 10,000 mAh and 300–450 g for 20,000 mAh. If you carry it daily, narrow by weight before capacity.
PSE mark
Since February 2019, power banks without the PSE mark cannot be sold in Japan (Electrical Appliances and Materials Safety Act). Avoid listings that do not show it.
Flying
Never in checked baggage, carry-on only. The usual rule: up to 100 Wh needs no approval, over 100 Wh up to 160 Wh needs airline approval (usually two at most), over 160 Wh is not allowed. Since 2025 more airlines ban using or charging power banks on board or limit how many you bring, and Japanese domestic flights ask you to keep them with you rather than in the overhead bin. Check the Wh on the label (20,000 mAh is about 74 Wh; the 27,000 mAh class is around 100 Wh and needs checking), and check your airline’s current rules.

Watch out for

  • Very cheap high-capacity units sometimes overstate their capacity.
  • Stop using one that swells, gets hot or smells, and take it to a municipal or JBRC collection point (not the regular trash).
  • Using it outside Japan: Works anywhere (USB). To take it home by air, it must go in your carry-on; check the Wh on the label.

How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n) … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50

So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.

Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
