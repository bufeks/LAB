How to choose

Unit price, single-ply equivalent
Double-ply is two sheets, so its paper equals twice the length of single-ply. Work out the total as rolls × length × (2 for double-ply) and compare.
Long rolls
“2× length” and “3× length” rolls cut the effort of changing rolls and the storage space. The roll is wider, so check that it fits your holder.
Softness
100% pulp is softer; recycled paper is cheaper and has a lower environmental impact.
Storage
Bulk packs are cheaper but need somewhere to go. Consider the size of the box that arrives.

Watch out for

  • Listings without a roll length cannot have a unit price calculated, so they are left out of the unit-price ranking.
  • Using it outside Japan: No special issue, but it is bulky, so it is mostly useful for people living in Japan.

How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n) … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50

So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.

Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
