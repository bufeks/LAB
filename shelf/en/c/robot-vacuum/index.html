How to choose

Mapping
Models that map the room with LiDAR (laser) or a camera move efficiently and let you set no-go zones and clean room by room.
Height
To clean under a sofa or bed, compare the gap under the furniture with the height of the robot.
Auto-empty
With models whose dock sucks out the dust, you only empty it once every few weeks. The dock is large, and dust bags cost money.
Mopping
Useful for mostly hard floors. If you have carpets, check whether it can lift its mop.
Consumables
Check the cost and availability of replacement brushes, filters and dust bags.

Watch out for

  • It can get caught on cables and small objects on the floor. You need the habit of tidying the floor before it runs.
  • For app-connected models, features may stop working if the maker ends the service.
  • Using it outside Japan: Before using it abroad, check whether the charging dock’s power adapter accepts 100–240 V; if it is 100 V only, do not use it on higher voltages.

How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n) … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50

So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.

Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
