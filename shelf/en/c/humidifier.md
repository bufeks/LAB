The type changes everything. For hygiene, steam (higher running cost); for low running cost, evaporative; for size and price, ultrasonic (only with regular cleaning). Choose a rated room size a step larger than your room.

## How to choose

- **Steam**: Boils the water, so it is hygienic and humidifies strongly. Uses a few hundred watts, and some models have a hot outlet.
- **Evaporative**: Blows air through a wet filter: low power and quiet. Output depends on room temperature and humidity, and the filter needs regular cleaning and replacement.
- **Ultrasonic**: Cheap, small and quiet. It turns the water straight into mist, so a neglected tank spreads bacteria, and minerals in tap water can leave white dust.
- **Hybrid**: Adds a heater to an evaporative or ultrasonic design to balance output and power use. More expensive.
- **Room size**: Two figures are given, for a wooden Japanese-style room and a prefab Western-style room; the wooden-room figure is smaller. Compare using the one that matches your home.

### Watch out for

- Check the price and availability of replacement filters (running cost) first.
- A tank with a narrow opening is hard to clean, which matters for hygiene.
- **Using it outside Japan**: Made for Japan’s 100 V mains, and the manufacturer supports use in Japan only. 110–120 V is above its rating and can cause overheating or failure; never use it on 220–240 V.

## How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

- Formula: `score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n)  … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50`
- Why: So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.
- Price check: Prices are recorded daily and compared only with days on the same shipping terms. Under 7 days observed: insufficient_data. At the lowest observed price and at least 3% under the median: lowest_observed. At or under 90% of the median: below_usual. At or over 110%: above_usual. Otherwise usual. The window is up to 90 days and reported as window_days.
- Note: Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
