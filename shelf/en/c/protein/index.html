How to choose

Price per kg
Sizes differ (1 kg, 3 kg, 1 kg × 2 and so on), so divide by the total weight. Larger sizes are often cheaper.
Type
Whey comes from milk and is absorbed quickly. WPI is a highly refined whey with less lactose, for people whose stomach is easily upset. Soy comes from soybeans and is absorbed slowly.
Protein content
The amount of protein in 1 kg differs between products. Comparing the cost per gram of protein, from the protein per serving and the price, is the fairest.
Sweeteners and additives
For people who want to avoid artificial sweeteners there are additive-free and plain types. They taste less good but are easier to use in cooking.

Watch out for

  • For competitive athletes there is a risk of contamination with substances covered by doping tests. Check for third-party certification (such as Informed Choice).
  • People with a medical condition or a limit on protein intake should consult a doctor.
  • Using it outside Japan: Many countries restrict bringing in supplements or food containing milk or meat products; check your country’s import rules before taking it home.

How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n) … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50

So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.

Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
