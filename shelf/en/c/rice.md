## How to choose (as of 2026-10)

- **Price per kg**: Sizes vary (5 kg, 10 kg, 2 × 5 kg and so on), so compare the price divided by the total weight. For listings with shipping extra, recalculate with shipping included.
- **Crop year and milling date**: The newer the “Reiwa N” crop year, the closer to new-crop rice. Flavour fades after milling, so buying small amounts with a recent milling date tastes better.
- **Single-origin or blended**: “Single-origin rice” (tan’itsu genryōmai) contains only rice of the same region, variety and crop year. Blended rice is cheaper and usually tastes less good than brand rice.
- **Musenmai**: Can be cooked without rinsing, so there is no rinse water and you save time and water. Because part of the grain is removed in milling, it tends to cost a little more for the same weight.
- **Storage**: Keep it away from heat and humidity in an airtight container. In summer, the vegetable drawer of the fridge suits it. As a guide, buy an amount you can finish in about a month.

### Watch out for

- “5 kg × 2” and “10 kg” are the same amount. Separate bags are easier to store.
- Subscriptions and furusato nozei gifts are a different comparison (only ordinary purchases are compared here).
- **Using it outside Japan**: Many countries restrict bringing in rice; check your country’s import rules before taking it home.

## How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

- Formula: `score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n)  … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50`
- Why: So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.
- Price check: Prices are recorded daily and compared only with days on the same shipping terms. Under 7 days observed: insufficient_data. At the lowest observed price and at least 3% under the median: lowest_observed. At or under 90% of the median: below_usual. At or over 110%: above_usual. Otherwise usual. The window is up to 90 days and reported as window_days.
- Note: Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
