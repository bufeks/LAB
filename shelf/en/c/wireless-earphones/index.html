How to choose

Noise cancelling
Makes the biggest difference on trains, planes and in cafés. Strength varies a lot between models, so models with many reviews are the safer bet.
Codec
iPhone does not support LDAC or aptX; it tops out at AAC. On an Android phone that supports LDAC or aptX, earbuds with the same codec sound better.
Multipoint
Connects to two devices at once, so you can take a phone call during a computer meeting without re-pairing.
Water resistance
IPX4 or better handles sweat and light rain. For sport, IPX5 or better.
Battery life
“Up to N hours” usually includes recharges from the case. Check how long the earbuds last on their own.

Watch out for

  • Fit and isolation depend heavily on the shape of your ears. Check that ear tips in several sizes are included.
  • Using it outside Japan: Work anywhere. Charge them over USB.

How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n) … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50

So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.

Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
