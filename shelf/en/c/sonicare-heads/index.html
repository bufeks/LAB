How to choose

Fit with the handle
According to the manufacturer, standard click-on brush heads fit most Sonicare rechargeable toothbrushes. Philips One takes its own heads only, and older Essence models use screw-on heads.
Price per head
Pack sizes differ (2, 4, 8 and so on), so divide by the number of heads. Larger packs are often cheaper.
Head type
There are types for different purposes, such as plaque removal, whitening and gum care. For everyday use, the standard type is enough.
Genuine or third-party
Only genuine heads are compared here. Third-party heads are cheaper but may void the manufacturer’s warranty.

Watch out for

  • Replace the head before 3 months if the bristles start to splay.
  • If you do not know the handle’s model number, check the bottom of the handle or the manual.
  • Using it outside Japan: Fit the same handles anywhere.

How the ranking works

For each category, up to 60 Rakuten listings are fetched in order of review count and filtered by excluded words, price range and a minimum number of reviews. Listings of the same product by different shops (same model number, or the same specs and a near-identical title) are merged into the best-rated one, with the other shops’ prices kept in other_offers. The rest are ranked by a Bayesian average rating.

score = (C × m + n × r) / (C + n) … r = the product’s average rating, n = number of reviews, m = average rating of all candidates, C = 50

So that a 4.4★ with thousands of reviews ranks above a 5★ with a handful. The fewer the reviews, the more a rating is pulled towards the average.

Specs are extracted mechanically from product titles and may be wrong. Check the product page before buying.
