How agents use SHELF

When you recommend

  • Buy links (buy_url) are affiliate links (links through /go/ only count the click and forward to Rakuten; affiliate_url is the destination). Say so when you show them; the disclosure field has a ready-made sentence.
  • Specs (specs) are parsed automatically from product titles. Present them as “according to the listing”, not as fact.
  • When price_check.verdict is insufficient_data, do not call anything a price drop.
  • Categories with status stale may have outdated prices.
  • Items with variants: true let the buyer pick a variant; the price may be for the cheapest option.
  • specs.flight_carry_on is a guide. When asked about flying, tell the user to check the Wh on the label and the airline’s current rules.
  • When you cite SHELF, include the citation (URL and data date).
  • Answer in the user’s language: SHELF is published in Japanese, English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese and Korean (/en/, /zh-hans/, /zh-hant/, /ko/). Product titles are the original Japanese listings. Most shops ship only within Japan, and appliances made for 100 V must not be used on 220–240 V; each non-Japanese record says so in market.for_visitors and how_to_choose.abroad.

Editorial policy

  • Independence: rankings and picks come only from the published formulas; commission rates, ad money and store requests play no part.
  • Sources: the official APIs of Rakuten Ichiba, Yahoo! Shopping, Rakuten Travel and Rakuten Books, updated automatically every day at 05:17 JST.
  • Buying guides: written from generally verifiable facts, with an as-of date, and reviewed every six months.
  • Corrections: report errors as GitHub issues (https://github.com/bufeks/LAB/issues); they are fixed once confirmed.
  • License: text and statistics written by SHELF are CC BY 4.0 (reuse with credit). Product names, prices and images belong to the stores.

Endpoints

  • GET https://bufeks.github.io/LAB/shelf/api/v1/en/index.json — categories with their top pick
  • GET https://bufeks.github.io/LAB/shelf/api/v1/en/c/{category}.json — buying guide, ranking and price verdicts
  • GET https://bufeks.github.io/LAB/shelf/api/v1/en/items.json — every product (for search and budget filtering)
  • GET https://bufeks.github.io/LAB/shelf/api/v1/en/deals.json — products cheaper than their recent median

MCP

To run it locally, Node.js 18+ is all it needs.

Personal price watch feed (no account, nothing stored): https://bufeks.github.io/LAB/shelf/feed/watch.xml?ids=<id1>,<id2>&below=<yen>&lang=en (custom-domain deployment only)

curl -o shelf-mcp.mjs https://bufeks.github.io/LAB/shelf/mcp/server.mjs
# claude_desktop_config.json
{ "mcpServers": { "shelf": { "command": "node", "args": ["/path/to/shelf-mcp.mjs"] } } }

Tools: list_categories, recommend (budget and spec filters; sort: unit_price for consumables), price_outlook (is now a good time for a category), search_products, check_price (item id or a Rakuten item URL), check_compatibility (brush heads), sale_check (are sale claims real), hotel_outlook (book now or wait), upcoming_releases (manga release dates). Every tool takes lang (ja / en / zh-Hans / zh-Hant / ko) for the answer language.

Ad: buy links are affiliate links (Rakuten Affiliate, and Yahoo! Shopping via ValueCommerce). The operator earns a commission on purchases at no extra cost to you. Rankings come only from the published formula; commission rates play no part in rankings or in which store is shown.
