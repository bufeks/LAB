Hotel prices: book now or wait? (2 adults, 1 room, 1 night)

For the coming weekends in major areas, SHELF records the lowest price of each hotel bookable on Rakuten Travel every day, and says whether prices for that night are rising or falling.

Prices are the cheapest plan for 2 adults in 1 room for 1 night, as shown by Rakuten Travel. The area level is the median of each available hotel’s lowest price. Fewer hotels found means rooms are selling out.

How the ranking works

For each night, the area price level is recorded daily. At least 5% above the median of earlier records is rising; at least 5% below is falling. Past movement does not guarantee future prices.
