How the ranking works

Each item is compared with its own price history (the median over up to 90 days, on the same shipping terms), never with a “regular price” the shop states. At least 5% below the median and at least half the claimed discount (a quarter for “up to”) is genuine; at least 3% is smaller than claimed; less is not lower than usual. Coupons are not in the listed price and are shown separately. Under 7 days of history is unverified.
